Our story

Built for people who
build their own setup.

Buyora started with a simple idea: buying a laptop or a keyboard online shouldn't mean guessing at specs or wondering if the deal is real. No clutter, no guesswork — just the computer gear worth having, delivered fast.

Started in a spare bedroom. Grown by word of mouth.

We began in 2021 with a handful of laptops and gaming accessories and a promise to never sell anything we wouldn't use ourselves. Since then, Buyora has grown into a destination for gaming laptops, graphics cards, monitors, keyboards, mice, headsets, and mouse pads — but that promise hasn't changed.

Every spec sheet on Buyora is checked before it's listed, and every order is backed by real people who answer the phone.
